<p class="ltx_p"><math id="S0.I2.i1.p1.m1" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\bigstar" display="inline"><mi mathvariant="normal">★</mi></math> <span class="ltx_text ltx_font_bold">Def.</span> For <math id="S0.I2.i1.p1.m2" class="ltx_Math" alttext="Y\subset X" display="inline"><mrow><mi>Y</mi><mo>⊂</mo><mi>X</mi></mrow></math>:   <math id="S0.I2.i1.p1.m3" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\mathcal{T}_{Y}=\{Y\cap U\mid U\in\mathcal{T}\}" display="inline"><mrow><msub><mi class="ltx_font_mathcaligraphic">𝒯</mi><mi>Y</mi></msub><mo>=</mo><mrow><mo stretchy="false">{</mo><mrow><mi>Y</mi><mo>∩</mo><mi>U</mi></mrow><mo fence="true" lspace="0em" rspace="0em">∣</mo><mrow><mi>U</mi><mo>∈</mo><mi class="ltx_font_mathcaligraphic">𝒯</mi></mrow><mo stretchy="false">}</mo></mrow></mrow></math>. <math id="S0.I2.i1.p1.m5" class="ltx_Math" alttext="(Y,\mathcal{T}_{Y})" display="inline"><mrow><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>Y</mi><mo>,</mo><msub><mi class="ltx_font_mathcaligraphic">𝒯</mi><mi>Y</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow></math> is a <em class="ltx_emph ltx_font_italic">subspace</em>.</p>

<p class="ltx_p"><span class="ltx_text ltx_font_bold ltx_font_italic" style="font-size:90%;color:#3D3D3D;">Proof.<span class="ltx_text ltx_font_medium ltx_font_upright"> All three axioms come from distributivity:
<math id="S0.I2.i1.p2.m1" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\bigcup(Y\cap U_{\alpha})=Y\cap\bigcup U_{\alpha}" display="inline"><mrow><mrow><mo mathcolor="#3D3D3D" rspace="0em">⋃</mo><mrow><mo mathcolor="#3D3D3D" stretchy="false">(</mo><mrow><mi mathcolor="#3D3D3D">Y</mi><mo mathcolor="#3D3D3D">∩</mo><msub><mi mathcolor="#3D3D3D">U</mi><mi mathcolor="#3D3D3D">α</mi></msub></mrow><mo mathcolor="#3D3D3D" stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow></mrow><mo mathcolor="#3D3D3D">=</mo><mrow><mi mathcolor="#3D3D3D">Y</mi><mo mathcolor="#3D3D3D" rspace="0.055em">∩</mo><mrow><mo mathcolor="#3D3D3D">⋃</mo><msub><mi mathcolor="#3D3D3D">U</mi><mi mathcolor="#3D3D3D">α</mi></msub></mrow></mrow></mrow></math>, likewise for finite <math id="S0.I2.i1.p2.m2" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\cap" display="inline"><mo mathcolor="#3D3D3D">∩</mo></math>.</span></span></p>
</div>

<em class="ltx_emph ltx_font_italic">open in <math id="S0.I2.i2.p1.m3" class="ltx_Math" alttext="Y" display="inline"><mi>Y</mi></math></em> or <em class="ltx_emph ltx_font_italic">open in <math id="S0.I2.i2.p1.m4" class="ltx_Math" alttext="X" display="inline"><mi>X</mi></math></em>.</p>
